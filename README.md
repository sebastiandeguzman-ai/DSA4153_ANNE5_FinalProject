# Functional Literacy of Filipinos by Region, Sex, and Age Group: A 2019 and 2024 Comparison

## Overview
While national literacy statistics often present a single unified figure for the Philippines, this broad metric obscures critical sub-national disparities. This project analyzes functional literacy rates across the country by breaking down data across three core dimensions: **Region**, **Sex**, and **Age Group**, comparing results between **2019** and **2024**.

By identifying specific regions and demographic groups experiencing lower functional literacy levels, this analysis aims to provide actionable insights for decision-makers, educators, and policy advocates.

---

## Project Context & Problem Statement
* **Functional Literacy Definition:** The ability of a person to read, write, compute, and comprehend. In addition to basic literacy skills, functional literacy includes a higher level of comprehension skills, such as integrating two or more pieces of information and making inferences based on the given information.
* **Problem:** Literacy in the Philippines is often described with a single national figure, but this hides large differences between regions, sexes, and age groups. Education practitioners and Department of Education (DepEd) staff need to see where literacy is lowest in order to decide which areas and groups need the most attention, yet this information is not easy to see or compare across years.
* **Target Audience:** Students, Education Practitioners, and Department of Education (DepEd) Employees.

---

## Key Analytical Questions
1. Which regions have the highest and lowest functional literacy rates in 2024, and how large is the gap between them?
2. How does functional literacy differ by sex and age group in 2024, and in which regions is the gap between males and females widest?
3. Using matching definitions, how do regional rankings differ between 2019 and 2024, and which regions stay among the lowest in both years?
